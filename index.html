What GEO means here

Generative engine optimization, in plain words.

AI answers are built from sources the AI can reach and trust. GEO is the work of making your business one of those clear, reachable, consistent sources.

01 · Reachable

Can AI tools reach your site?

We check indexing, robots.txt rules for search crawlers like Googlebot, Bingbot and OAI-SearchBot, firewall blocks, and whether key text is real text rather than images.

02 · Understandable

Is it clear what you do?

Services, areas served, prices, hours and credentials should be stated directly on the page, so a customer's specific question has a specific answer.

03 · Consistent

Do other sources agree?

Your name, phone, location and services should match across your website, Google Business Profile, directories and social profiles.

We follow the platforms' own guidance. Google states that there are no additional requirements or special optimizations needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond SEO fundamentals, and that no special schema markup or hidden GEO trick guarantees inclusion. OpenAI says sites that opt out of OAI-SearchBot won't be shown in ChatGPT search answers, although other types of links may still appear.

So the work is practical: improve discoverability, clarity and consistency, then measure what actually changes. We don't sell hidden tricks.

Process

Four steps, all in writing.

  1. Brief

    A 20-minute call or WhatsApp chat. We agree your key services, locations and the questions customers really ask.

  2. Test

    We run your prompt set across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot and record every answer and cited source.

  3. Diagnose

    We check your site, profiles and directories, then rank every issue by impact and effort.

  4. Plan and measure

    You receive a prioritised plan your team can act on, and we walk you through it on a call. With the Implementation Guide you also get numbered fix instructions, and we re-test the same prompts after 30 days to see what changed.

FAQ

Questions we're asked most.

What exactly do I get?

A written review of where your website, local search presence and publicly available business information leave opportunities for improvement, a prioritised plan your team can act on, and a walkthrough call. We then re-test the same prompts to measure what changed.

Is GEO different from SEO?

They overlap a lot. Google says its AI features are built on its core search systems, so SEO fundamentals still apply. GEO adds a focus on how AI tools describe you, which sources they cite, and whether your business facts are clear and consistent enough to be quoted.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend us?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. AI answers vary by user, location, wording and time, and Google says no special schema markup or hidden trick guarantees inclusion. What we can do is show where your website, local search presence and public business information leave opportunities for improvement, give you a prioritised plan, then measure the same prompts again.

Do you make the changes on our website?

Our services are audits and step-by-step instructions. You, your team or your developer make the changes. Instructions are written for beginners, with screenshots or exact menu paths where possible. Compare the packages.

Can you work with businesses in my country?

Yes. The work is remote by nature: we test AI tools, review your website and public profiles, and write instructions. For local results we test prompts that mention your city or area, with the location set to your market where the tool allows it. We currently work in English.

How do you measure progress?

We re-run the same documented prompt set and compare mentions, accuracy and cited sources. Where you give us access, we also check Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, which now has an AI Performance report showing citations in Copilot and Bing AI summaries.

What do you need from us?

Your website address, your main services and locations, links to your Google Business Profile and social profiles, and 10 to 20 questions customers ask you. Access to Search Console is helpful but optional. See the four-step process or the sample audit.
