Frequently asked questions

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a business’s information clear, consistent and well structured, so AI-powered tools and search engines can find, understand and reference it accurately.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on helping pages rank in search results. GEO builds on the same foundations (technical health, useful content, trustworthy business information) and also considers how AI tools may summarize and cite a business. In practice the two overlap heavily.

What does a GEO audit include?

We review technical and on-page basics, how your services are explained, business information consistency and, where relevant, structured data. You receive evidence-based findings and a prioritized list of recommendations. The exact scope is confirmed before work begins.

Does GEO guarantee that AI tools will recommend my business?

No. Search engines and AI tools are controlled by third parties, and their results change. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings or recommendations. Our aim is to remove avoidable barriers and make your business easier to understand.

Can I improve visibility without rebuilding my website?

Often, yes. Clearer service pages, corrected business details, better headings and added structured data can usually be done on an existing site. If something larger is needed, we’ll say so and explain why.

How does the process work?

We agree scope, audit your website and visibility, share evidence-based findings, prioritize recommendations, then provide implementation guidance or agreed support. Steps can vary by project.
